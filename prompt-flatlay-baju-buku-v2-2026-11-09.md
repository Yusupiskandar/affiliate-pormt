# Prompt Google Flow – Versi 2: Flat Lay Baju + Tumpukan Buku

Turunan dari prompt terkunci di `prompt-flatlay-baju-tangan-2026-11-09.md` (bagian 3). Perubahan: papan kayu @cupy155 diganti tumpukan buku jurnal + pulpen, ditambah larangan kaki/sepatu masuk frame.

Mode: **Ingredients to Video**, rasio **9:16**, Veo 3.1 (Fast untuk tes, Quality untuk final), 2–4 output.
Upload: **foto baju** (ganti tiap produk). Prompt tidak perlu diubah.

```
Vertical 9:16 photorealistic smartphone video, fixed top-down bird's-eye view, camera completely still on a tripod.

PRODUCT: the clothing shown in the uploaded product reference image. Show exactly and only the items that appear in that image, nothing added and nothing removed: if the reference shows only a top, there is no bottom garment; if it shows a set, show the full set. Keep the exact colors, fabric texture, pattern, embroidery, print, collar, buttons, pockets, sleeves and proportions identical to the reference image; the design never changes. The clothing is laid flat, neat and symmetrical in the center of the frame on a textured medium-grey carpet, with some empty carpet visible around it.

SETTING: in the top-right corner a round matte black flower pot with broad green leaves and many small clustered orange-peach and cream flowers. In the top-left corner a small neat stack of notebooks lying on the carpet, slightly angled: on top a closed dark slate-blue leather journal with a plain cover and a thin embossed border, beneath it a cream-paper notebook peeking out with faint pencil sketches on its pages, and at the bottom a black leather notebook with an elastic band; a black fountain pen with a silver clip rests diagonally across the top journal. Soft even natural daylight, no harsh shadows.

TALENT: only one woman's right hand and forearm appears, entering from the right edge, wearing a burgundy maroon knit sweater sleeve; face and body are never shown.

ACTION:
0-1s: The clothing is already laid out perfectly flat; the hand slides in from the right edge of the frame.
1-3s: The flat open palm gently smooths the front of the main garment from the center outward, fabric slightly flattening under the hand.
3-5s: The hand smooths the sleeves and then glides down along the garment toward the hem, straightening a small crease; it only touches items that exist in the reference.
5-7s: The hand moves up, lightly tidies the collar or neckline and shoulder with the fingertips, then slowly slides out of frame to the right.
7-8s: The hand is gone; the neatly arranged clothing stays perfectly still in the center.

CAMERA: locked-off static overhead shot, no zoom, no camera movement, the whole product always fully visible in frame.
AUDIO: no music at all. Only quiet natural room ambience and the soft rustle of fabric when the hand touches the cloth. No voice, no speech, no singing.
REALISM: realistic fabric physics and texture, true-to-life colors matching the reference, natural hand with five fingers and relaxed slow movements, subtle phone camera grain, authentic UGC product video. The video is clean with no graphics or text on top.

Avoid: floating watermark, app watermark overlay, username overlay, logo stickers on screen, on-screen graphics, subtitles, captions, text or titles on the book covers, any garment or accessory not in the reference image, changing the clothing design or color, background music, a second hand, showing a face, body, legs, feet or shoes, distorted fingers, camera shake, zooming, the books moving.
```

## Catatan
- **@cupy155 tidak ada lagi di video** karena papannya diganti buku. Kalau tetap ingin handle tampil, tambahkan teks "@cupy155" kecil di CapCut, misalnya di pojok bawah.
