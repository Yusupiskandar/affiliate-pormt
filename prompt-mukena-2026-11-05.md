# Paket Prompt Google Flow: Mukena Satin Jacquard Dusty Pink

Referensi: video TikTok mukena (30 dtk, 9:16, musik latar tanpa narasi).
Target: 4 klip × 8 detik di Google Flow (Veo 3.1), lalu disunting jadi ±30 detik.

---

## 1. Ringkasan Referensi

| Detik | Adegan | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | **Hook:** merapikan hijab dekat pipi, lalu melambai ke kamera | Medium close-up |
| 2–4 | Mundur, membuka tangan memamerkan kain | Medium shot |
| 4–12 | Memegang tas pouch serasi, bergoyang pelan, melangkah di tempat | Full body |
| 12,5 | *Dip to black* | – |
| 13–14 | Mengangkat lengan, memperlihatkan renda bordir | Medium close-up |
| 14,5 | *Dip to black* | – |
| 15–18 | Pegang hijab, rentangkan kain, angkat tas di dekat wajah | Medium close-up |
| 18,8 & 20,5 | *Dip to black* | – |
| 21–27 | Pose: tangan di dagu, mata terpejam, tangan di pipi, melambai | Medium close-up |
| 27–30 | Senyum ke kamera, musik berhenti | Medium close-up |

**Kamera:** HP di tripod setinggi dada, diam, tanpa zoom.
**Setting:** kamar apartemen minimalis dengan lemari kayu terang, cermin, kasur putih, lantai kayu, lampu LED hangat di plafon, dan cahaya siang dari jendela.
**Audio:** musik latar lembut sepanjang video, tanpa bicara.

## 4. Prompt per Klip

> Pengaturan Flow: **Veo 3.1**, rasio **9:16**, output **4** per prompt, mode **Ingredients to Video** (unggah Ingredient 1–3).
> Pakai **Fast** untuk tes, **Quality** untuk hasil final.

### Klip 1: Hook (video final 0–8 dtk)
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic UGC smartphone footage shot on iPhone, static tripod at chest height.
A young Indonesian woman in her early 20s with soft glam makeup, rosy cheeks, glossy pink lips and a sweet gentle smile, wearing a dusty pink satin jacquard mukena (Muslim prayer outfit covering head to feet) with a shimmering embossed floral pattern, wide scalloped embroidered floral lace trim along the edges and sleeves, and tiny beaded pom-pom edging framing her face. She wears a thin gold watch and delicate rings. Setting: minimalist apartment bedroom with light wood wardrobe, a mirror panel, white bed, vertical wood slat wall, light wooden floor, warm LED cove ceiling light mixed with soft natural daylight.
Action: 0-2s medium close-up, she gently adjusts the edge of the hijab near her cheek and looks into the camera. 2-4s she smiles and gives a small friendly wave. 4-8s she takes two steps back into a full body shot, opens her arms slightly so the wide sleeves and lace trim drape and sway, the satin sheen catching the light.
Camera: static, eye-level, no zoom.
Audio: soft cheerful feminine background music, light room ambience, no speech.
Realism: natural skin texture, realistic fabric physics and drape, true-to-life colors, subtle phone camera grain.
Avoid: text overlay, watermark, distorted hands, extra fingers, plastic skin, pattern changing.
```

## 5. Panduan Editing (CapCut)

1. Susun Klip 1 → 2 → 3 → 4. Potong bagian yang kaku, targetkan total **28–30 detik**.
2. **Dip to black** (0,2–0,3 detik) seperti di referensi: di antara Klip 2→3, di tengah Klip 3 (setelah pamer kain), dan di antara Klip 3→4.
3. **Musik:** matikan audio Veo kalau tidak serasi. Ganti dengan sound yang sedang tren di TikTok, dan sesuaikan transisi dengan beat.
4. **Teks overlay** (tambahkan di CapCut, jangan di-generate):
   - 0–3 dtk (hook): *"Mukena satin yang bikin adem & cantik pas sholat 🌸"*
   - Saat close-up renda: *"Detail renda bordir + manik di wajah"*
   - Saat tas: *"Free tas pouch serasi!"* (tulis hanya kalau memang termasuk paket)
   - Akhir: *"Cek keranjang kuning 💛"*
5. Pasang link produk di keranjang kuning saat posting.

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Masalah | Solusi |
|---|---|
| Motif / warna mukena berubah tiap klip | Wajib pakai **Ingredients** foto produk asli; ulangi Lock identik |
| Wajah talent beda antar klip | Pakai foto talent yang sama, atau mode **Frames to Video** dengan frame terakhir klip sebelumnya sebagai frame awal |
| Tangan / jari aneh | Sederhanakan gerakan tangan, pilih yang terbaik dari 4 output |
| Muncul teks / tulisan acak | Pastikan baris *Avoid* ada, lalu crop atau tutup di editor |
| Gerakan terlalu cepat | Tambahkan `slow, graceful, relaxed movements` di bagian Action |
