# Prompt Google Flow: Flat Lay Baju + Tangan Merapikan (Satu Prompt, Produk Bisa Diganti)

Referensi: Haviza_video_no_watermark.mp4 (10 dtk, 9:16, satu take, kamera diam dari atas)
Versi ini: **satu prompt 8 detik**, **tanpa musik** (hanya suara kain), bunga diganti pot bunga oranye-krem, papan bertuliskan logo TikTok + @cupy155.

---

## 1. Ringkasan Referensi

**Format:** vertikal 9:16, satu take tanpa cut, kamera diam dari atas (top-down).
**Talent:** tidak tampil wajah, hanya satu tangan wanita berlengan sweater rajut maroon yang masuk dari kanan.
**Setting:** baju ditata flat lay di karpet abu-abu, pot bunga di pojok kanan atas, papan kecil di pojok kiri atas.
**Lighting:** cahaya siang lembut dan rata.

| Detik | Aksi |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Baju sudah tertata, tangan masuk dari kanan |
| 1–3 | Tangan mengusap depan atasan |
| 3–5 | Tangan merapikan bawahan (celana/rok/bagian bawah gamis) |
| 5–7 | Tangan merapikan kerah & bahu, lalu keluar frame |
| 7–8 | Baju diam rapi tanpa tangan (frame penutup) |

---

## 2. Persiapan Ingredients (mode *Ingredients to Video*)

- **Ingredient 1 – Foto produk (WAJIB, ganti tiap produk):** foto flat lay baju dari atas, latar polos, terang, seluruh set terlihat, tanpa teks/logo/watermark seller.
- **Ingredient 2 – Foto setting (dibuat sekali, dipakai untuk semua produk):** karpet abu-abu + pot bunga + papan @cupy155. Buat dengan prompt di bawah.
- **Ingredient 3 – Foto tangan (opsional).**

> Jangan pakai foto bunga dari Vecteezy langsung sebagai ingredient (ada watermark). Bunganya sudah dideskripsikan di prompt di bawah.

### Prompt gambar Ingredient 2 (setting) — buat di generator gambar Flow
```
Top-down bird's-eye photo of an empty textured medium-grey carpet floor. In the top-right corner: a round matte black flower pot seen from above, filled with lush broad green leaves and many small clustered double-petal flowers in soft orange-peach with cream-white edges, like kalanchoe blossoms, a few thin stems with tiny buds spreading outward. In the top-left corner: a small square light-wood board with a white painted surface showing the TikTok music-note logo followed by the text "@cupy155" in clean black handwritten-style lettering. Large empty space in the center for a clothing flat lay. Soft even daylight from a window, no harsh shadows, photorealistic, vertical 9:16, no other text, no watermark.
```
Generate beberapa kali dan pilih yang tulisan **@cupy155** terbaca benar. Kalau tulisan atau logonya selalu salah, buat papannya kosong (hapus kalimat logo & teks), lalu tambahkan logo TikTok + @cupy155 di CapCut (lihat bagian Editing).

### Prompt gambar Ingredient 3 (tangan, opsional)
```
Top-down photo of a woman's right hand and forearm reaching in from the right side, wearing a burgundy maroon cable-knit sweater sleeve with a small open-knit pattern, natural relaxed hand with five fingers, clean short nails, on a plain textured grey carpet, soft daylight, photorealistic, no text.
```

### Kalau foto produk dari seller dipakai model / digantung
```
Turn the clothing in the reference image into a neat top-down flat lay on a textured medium-grey carpet, garment laid perfectly flat and symmetrical, keep the exact same colors, fabric, pattern, embroidery, buttons, collar and all details of the original garment, soft even daylight, photorealistic product photo, vertical 9:16, no text, no logo, no mannequin, no person.
```

## 3. Prompt Video (satu prompt universal, 8 detik)

Mode: **Ingredients to Video**
- Upload **foto baju** (wajib, ganti tiap produk).
- Upload **foto setting** (Ingredient 2) juga kalau ada, supaya papan & bunga selalu sama. Tanpa foto setting pun prompt ini tetap bisa dipakai.

Prompt ini **tidak perlu diubah** saat ganti produk. Baju diambil dari foto yang Anda upload: kalau fotonya hanya kemeja, yang muncul hanya kemeja; kalau fotonya setelan, yang muncul setelan.

```
Vertical 9:16 photorealistic smartphone video, fixed top-down bird's-eye view, camera completely still on a tripod.

PRODUCT: the clothing shown in the uploaded product reference image. Show exactly and only the items that appear in that image, nothing added and nothing removed: if the reference shows only a top, there is no bottom garment; if it shows a set, show the full set. Keep the exact colors, fabric texture, pattern, embroidery, print, collar, buttons, pockets, sleeves and proportions identical to the reference image; the design never changes. The clothing is laid flat, neat and symmetrical in the center of the frame on a textured medium-grey carpet, with some empty carpet visible around it.

SETTING: in the top-right corner a round matte black flower pot with broad green leaves and many small clustered orange-peach and cream flowers. In the top-left corner a small square light-wood board lying on the carpet; the TikTok music-note logo and the handle "@cupy155" are laser-engraved directly into the wood surface in dark burnt-brown, as a physical part of the board, slightly angled with the board. If a setting reference image is provided, match it exactly. Soft even natural daylight, no harsh shadows.

TALENT: only one woman's right hand and forearm appears, entering from the right edge, wearing a burgundy maroon knit sweater sleeve; face and body are never shown.

ACTION:
0-1s: The clothing is already laid out perfectly flat; the hand slides in from the right edge of the frame.
1-3s: The flat open palm gently smooths the front of the main garment from the center outward, fabric slightly flattening under the hand.
3-5s: The hand smooths the sleeves and then glides down along the garment toward the hem, straightening a small crease; it only touches items that exist in the reference.
5-7s: The hand moves up, lightly tidies the collar or neckline and shoulder with the fingertips, then slowly slides out of frame to the right.
7-8s: The hand is gone; the neatly arranged clothing stays perfectly still in the center.

CAMERA: locked-off static overhead shot, no zoom, no camera movement, the whole product always fully visible in frame.
AUDIO: no music at all. Only quiet natural room ambience and the soft rustle of fabric when the hand touches the cloth. No voice, no speech, no singing.
REALISM: realistic fabric physics and texture, true-to-life colors matching the reference, natural hand with five fingers and relaxed slow movements, subtle phone camera grain, authentic UGC product video. The engraving on the wooden board is the only branding in the entire video; the video itself is clean with no graphics on top.

Avoid: floating watermark, TikTok app watermark overlay, username overlay, logo stickers on screen, on-screen graphics, subtitles, captions, any garment or accessory not in the reference image, changing the clothing design or color, background music, a second hand, showing a face or body, distorted fingers, camera shake, zooming, changing the board.
```

### Cara ganti produk
1. Upload foto baju baru bersama prompt di atas (plus foto setting kalau ada). Prompt tidak diubah.
2. Foto baju sebaiknya: tampak depan, latar polos, hanya produk yang dijual (tanpa model, tanpa teks/watermark seller). Barang yang terlihat di foto = barang yang akan muncul di video.
3. (Opsional) Kalau motif sering meleset, tambahkan satu kalimat deskripsi tepat setelah `PRODUCT:`, mis. `It is a men's white short-sleeve koko shirt with mandarin collar, black buttons and silver-grey diamond embroidery on the chest.`

## 4. Pengaturan Flow
- Model: **Veo 3.1 Fast** untuk tes, **Veo 3.1 Quality** untuk final.
- Rasio: **9:16 (portrait)**.
- Output per prompt: **2–4**, pilih yang baju & tulisan papannya paling sesuai.

## 5. Panduan Editing (CapCut)
1. Video 8 detik bisa langsung dipakai. Kalau ingin 10 detik seperti referensi: perlambat ke **0,8x**, atau freeze frame terakhir 2 detik.
2. Audio: biarkan audio asli (suara kain). Kalau Veo tetap menyelipkan musik, kecilkan/mute audio lalu tambahkan efek suara kain dari library CapCut.
3. Kalau tulisan papan dari Veo salah/berubah: tempel stiker logo TikTok + teks "@cupy155" tepat di atas papan kayu (bukan melayang di tengah layar), lalu kunci posisinya.
4. Kalau watermark TikTok melayang tetap muncul: tutup dengan blur/mosaic kecil di area itu, atau crop.
5. Teks harga / "cek keranjang kuning" di detik terakhir, di area bawah frame supaya tidak menutupi baju.

## 6. Troubleshooting
- **Muncul celana/bawahan padahal produk hanya atasan** → pastikan foto produk hanya berisi kemeja; tambahkan `It is a single top only, no bottom garment.` setelah `PRODUCT:`; generate ulang. Kalau masih muncul, buat dulu gambar flat lay kemeja di setting (Ingredient 2) dengan generator gambar, lalu pakai mode **Frames to Video** dengan gambar itu sebagai frame awal.
- **Muncul watermark TikTok melayang di layar** → generate ulang (pilih dari 2–4 output); kalau tetap muncul, tutup dengan blur kecil di CapCut. Paling ampuh: pakai foto setting (Ingredient 2) yang papannya sudah bertuliskan @cupy155, lalu hapus kalimat `the TikTok music-note logo and the handle "@cupy155" are laser-engraved...` dari prompt dan ganti dengan `the board is identical to the setting reference image`.
- **Motif/warna baju berubah** → pakai foto flat lay yang jelas tanpa teks, tambahkan kalimat produk spesifik, generate 4 output.
- **Tulisan @cupy155 berubah/salah eja** → pastikan Ingredient 2 tulisannya sudah benar; kalau tetap salah, pakai papan kosong dan tambahkan teks di CapCut.
- **Masih ada musik** → tulis ulang di awal bagian AUDIO: `Silent video except for fabric rustle, absolutely no music.`
- **Baju bergeser saat diusap** → ganti "smooths" menjadi `lightly glides the palm over` dan tambahkan `the garment stays in place`.
- **Muncul tangan kedua / jari aneh** → sederhanakan aksi 5-7s jadi usapan telapak saja.
- **Kamera bergerak/zoom** → ulangi `locked-off static overhead tripod shot` di awal prompt.
